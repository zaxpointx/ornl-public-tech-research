## Walkthrough (plain language)
- **What it is:** A planned suite of machine-learning tools for the cybersecurity of distributed energy resources such as solar, batteries and EV charging. [KNOWN: 1]
- **The problem:** More connected devices on the grid means more ways in, and separate security tools each keep their own data. [KNOWN: 1]
- **What goes in:** Grid physical and communications data, later from utility partners such as EPB. [KNOWN: 1]
- **What happens:** One platform and shared data pool feed vulnerability analysis, attack detection, mitigation and recovery tools. [KNOWN: 1]
- **What comes out:** Information operators can act on. [KNOWN: 1]
- **Where humans act:** Grid operators make the decisions. [KNOWN: 1]
- **What it is not:** Not described as deployed; the story says it "will be demonstrated". [KNOWN: 1]
- **Example:** UNKNOWN beyond the story.
