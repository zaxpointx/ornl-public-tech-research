# Sweep 1: Discovery and Reconstruction

Target: ORNL public research portfolio, narrowed to hidden technical capabilities visible across public project fragments.
Boundary: public ORNL, DOE/OSTI, public job postings, public project pages, and public technology-transfer material. No private, classified, credentialed, or contact-based research.

Tracks run sequentially in Hermes: autonomous science/workflows; scientific instruments; quantum/grid; AI/HPC; technology transfer/history. No subagent concurrency was used in this pass.

Provisional findings:
- Workflow orchestration and autonomous/agentic science recur as technical capabilities, not merely support functions.
- ORNL connects AI and automation to physical instruments, HPC, networking, materials, biology, and grid systems.
- Public job postings can reveal implementation priorities that project summaries do not state directly.
- Technology-transfer catalogs and historical archives are high-value sources for obscure work.

Provisional only. Search results are leads, not final evidence.
