# ORNL Inside-Out Reconstruction: Final Report

**Method:** Mandatory two-sweep workflow using Hermes built-in web search and direct public-page retrieval. No DeepAPI or external credentials. Sweep 1 reconstructed the portfolio; Sweep 2 independently re-read, re-fetched, searched for disconfirmation, and repaired claims.

## Direct answer

ORNL’s public footprint reveals more than isolated scientific projects. When connected carefully, it exposes a recurring operating pattern: AI and automation are being attached to instruments, HPC, distributed workflows, communications networks, materials, biology, and grid systems. The evidence supports that as a bounded cross-source inference, not a claim about confidential work.

## What is inferred

- ORNL appears to be treating workflow orchestration and autonomous experimentation as research capabilities in their own right, not just administrative plumbing.
- The public record suggests a convergence between scientific instruments, AI models, HPC, and cross-facility workflow systems.
- Public job and technology-transfer material is likely to reveal operational priorities that polished project pages omit, but unavailable pages must be manually verified before stronger wording.

## What this is not

- It is not a reconstruction of classified programs.
- It is not proof of internal architecture, ownership, production deployment, or current maturity where sources do not say so.
- It is not complete coverage of ORNL.
