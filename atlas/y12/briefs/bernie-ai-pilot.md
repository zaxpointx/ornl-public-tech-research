# Bernie AI pilot at Y-12

- Source: NNSA FY 2025 Performance Evaluation Report, Objective 5.1 [1]; LLNL poster describing Bernie [2]
- Status: piloted in FY 2025. [KNOWN: 1]

## Walkthrough (plain language)
- **What happened:** CNS was the first site outside Lawrence Livermore to pilot the "Bernie" AI tool, showed it at the 2025 Y-12 infrastructure "Deep Dive", and demonstrated it to NNSA senior leaders. [KNOWN: 1]
- **What Bernie is (per LLNL):** An AI system for NNSA infrastructure planning that answers questions about facilities using agents, a semantic router, vector databases and existing enterprise data pipelines. The poster stresses no internet connection and red-teaming. [KNOWN: 2]
- **Why:** Aging facilities across the enterprise compete for repair money; the tool helps prioritize. [KNOWN: 2]
- **Unknown:** What Y-12 data it used and whether it stayed in use.

## What stands out (interpretation)
An agentic, retrieval-based AI tool running offline inside the weapons complex: the same pattern (agents + RAG + strict network isolation) Cayden can study and build in a home lab. [INFERRED]
