# CNS Network Operations Center / Security Operations Center (NOC/SOC)

- Site story: "Meet the NOC/SOC", 2022-07-13 [1]
- Job post: "Network/Security Operations Center", Y-12, Oak Ridge, on DOE.jobs (fetched 2026-11-22, listed about a week earlier) [2]
- Status: active (hiring in November 2026). [KNOWN: 2]
- Years visible: 2019 (founded) to 2026

## Walkthrough (plain language)
- **What it is:** The 24/7 team that watches CNS's network for outages and security threats. [KNOWN: 1, 2]
- **Where:** Physically based at Pantex in Texas, but run as an enterprise service for both Pantex and Y-12 (as of 2022). [KNOWN: 1] Whether it still serves Y-12 from Pantex after Pantex changed operators in late 2024 is UNKNOWN; the 2026 post is for an Oak Ridge position. [KNOWN: 2; INFERRED: some NOC/SOC staff now sit in Oak Ridge]
- **Size:** 14 people in 2022, founded 2019, part of the Information Solutions and Services (IS&S) IT Operations organization. [KNOWN: 1]
- **What the job asks for:** TCP/IP and server basics, firewalls, incident response, SIEM, IDS/IPS (Snort, Cisco, FortiGate, Firepower named), packet capture, Linux or Windows admin, scripting, and Network+, Security+ or Splunk certifications preferred. [KNOWN: 2]
- **Conditions:** 12-hour rotating shifts, 100 percent on site, ability to get a DOE Q clearance, U.S. citizenship, drug testing; some roles may need a counterintelligence polygraph. [KNOWN: 2]
- **Education:** Bachelor's in engineering, science or IT, **or eight or more years of relevant education, training and/or experience**. [KNOWN: 2]
- **Career ladder:** The 2022 story says more than 60 percent of the original staff moved up into other IT and cyber jobs. [KNOWN: 1]

## What stands out (interpretation)
This is the most realistic entry door into Y-12 cyber work for someone coming from a technical college: the skills list is exactly what a TCAT cybersecurity program plus Security+ and a home lab teach, and the post explicitly counts training and experience in place of a degree. [INFERRED]
