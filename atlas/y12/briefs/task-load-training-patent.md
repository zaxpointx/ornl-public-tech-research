# Patent: personalized learning via task load optimization

- US patent 11,538,352, 2022-12-27; assignees per OSTI: Y-12 and Avrio Analytics (Knoxville) [1]
- Status: granted patent. Licensing status UNKNOWN.

## Walkthrough (plain language)
- **What it is:** A method where one ML model predicts the ideal workload (task load) for a trainee in a simulator, a second model recommends training content and conditions to hit it, and biometric or performance data is used to check and adjust. [KNOWN: 1]
- **Why it fits Y-12:** Y-12 trains people for high-hazard work with simulators and VR (see the [digital twin brief](digital-twin-llm-maintenance.md)). [INFERRED]
- **Signal:** OSTI lists 143 Y-12 patents. Among the 40 most recent (2018 to 2023), this is the only one whose title is about software; the rest are materials, sensors, tooling and processes. [KNOWN: OSTI API listing; INFERRED: classification by title, see [signals](../signals.md)]
