# eDC/RO: Electronic Derivative Classifier / Reviewing Official

- OSTI software record: https://www.osti.gov/biblio/1357242 (2017-02-17), credited to CNS Y-12 Information Solutions staff and the classification office [1]
- Licensing book entry: yes (2025 edition) [2]
- Status: offered for partnering and licensing. [KNOWN: 2]

## Walkthrough (plain language)
- **What it is:** A web-based document management and routing system for sending documents to classification reviewers. [KNOWN: 1]
- **What happens:** Upload, notify the reviewer, track status, then place the required markings, footer and a digital signature on the document. Common Office and PDF formats are supported. [KNOWN: 1]
- **Why it matters:** Every document leaving a site like Y-12 needs review. Automating the routing reduces risk and delay. [KNOWN: 1, "reduces security risks and increases workflow efficiencies"]
- **Not covered here:** How classification decisions are made. Out of scope.
