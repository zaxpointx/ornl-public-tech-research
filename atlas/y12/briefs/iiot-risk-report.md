# Emerging Threats and Technology Investigation: IIoT risk and mitigation for nuclear infrastructure

- OSTI: https://www.osti.gov/biblio/1893157, DOI 10.2172/1893157, report IROS65120, 2022-07-01 [1]
- Sponsor: NNSA. Author affiliations: Y-12, Avrio Analytics, Idaho National Laboratory, Pacific Northwest National Laboratory. [KNOWN: 1]

## Walkthrough (plain language)
- **What it is:** A technical report on the security risks when the nuclear industry adopts Internet of Things and Industrial IoT devices. [KNOWN: 1]
- **Key idea from the abstract:** IIoT does not replace classic SCADA process control; it adds sensors and AI/ML analytics on top, and that extra connectivity brings new risk. It leans on NIST guidance. [KNOWN: 1]
- **Unknown:** Specific recommendations were not summarized here beyond the abstract.

## What stands out (interpretation)
It pairs with Y-12's digital twin and sensor work: the same sensors that make predictive maintenance possible are the attack surface this report worries about. [INFERRED]
