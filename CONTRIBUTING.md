# Corrections and contributions

Accuracy matters more than volume here. If something is wrong, out of date, or overstated, please say so.

## Reporting an error
Open an issue using the **Correction** template, and include:
- the file and line (or claim) in question,
- what you believe is correct,
- a public source that supports it (URL).

Corrections are re-checked against the source, fixed in place, and logged in `verification/log.md` with the verdict.

## Ground rules for contributions
- Public sources only. No restricted, CUI, export-controlled, or internal material, and nothing obtained by logging in.
- Write in your own words. Quotes under 25 words, always cited.
- Tag every claim `[KNOWN]`, `[CORROBORATED]`, `[INFERRED]`, `[UNKNOWN]`, or `[UNRELATED]` (see `RULES.md`).
- Do not claim or imply ORNL affiliation.
- Do not add personal information about individuals (names, emails, handles, profiles). Describe roles instead.

## Takedown requests
If you are a rights holder or a person mentioned here and want something removed, open an issue or contact the maintainer through GitHub. Requests are handled promptly.
