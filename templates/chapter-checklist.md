# Chapter completion checklist

Use this checklist for every book chapter, deep dive, or major synthesis.

## Editorial

- [ ] The chapter has one clear thesis and a defined audience.
- [ ] Scope and exclusions are stated at the beginning.
- [ ] Terms are defined on first use and linked to a glossary when needed.
- [ ] The chapter has a short plain-English summary and a concrete example.
- [ ] Links to related chapters, briefs, maps, and ledgers are current.

## Evidence

- [ ] Every load-bearing claim has a source.
- [ ] Claims are labeled `KNOWN`, `CORROBORATED`, `INFERRED`, `UNKNOWN`, or `UNRELATED`.
- [ ] Primary sources are preferred; secondary sources are marked as leads or context.
- [ ] Citations include a stable URL and a retrieval date; page, section, or commit locators are included where available.
- [ ] Contradictions and negative searches are recorded rather than silently resolved.

## Reconstruction

- [ ] Inputs, processing, outputs, interfaces, human review, and maturity are described only where evidence supports them.
- [ ] Related projects are separated from direct dependencies and thematic similarities.
- [ ] The chapter does not turn a repository into proof of production deployment or institutional ownership.
- [ ] Unknowns explain what evidence would be needed to resolve them.

## Verification

- [ ] A second reading sweep re-fetches load-bearing sources.
- [ ] An adversarial pass searches for disconfirming evidence and overstatement.
- [ ] Counts and status labels agree with the catalog and validation script.
- [ ] The chapter appears in the Book Plan and relevant index pages.
