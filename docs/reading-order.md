# Reading order

1. [INDEX.md](../INDEX.md): every project by domain, with verification status.
2. [RULES.md](../RULES.md): evidence tags and scope rules.
3. [batches/](../batches/): per-batch summaries, oldest first.
4. [projects/](../projects/): one plain-English brief per project.
5. [verification/log.md](../verification/log.md): every fact check.
6. [connections/map.md](../connections/map.md): how projects relate.
7. [discovery/catalog.csv](../discovery/catalog.csv): master list.
8. [deep-dives/](../deep-dives/): full reconstructions (Cyberwheel).

The research was produced by a small pipeline of AI research assistants (discovery, reading, fact checking, connections, organizing) directed and reviewed by the maintainer. Role names such as "Scout", "Reader", or "Fact Checker" in the files refer to those pipeline stages.
