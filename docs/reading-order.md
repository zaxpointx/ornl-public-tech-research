# Reading order

1. [LAB-TREE.md](../LAB-TREE.md): how ORNL is organized and where each project sits.
2. [deep-dives/](../deep-dives/): full walkthroughs of Cyberwheel, the SNS neutron data pipeline, and the OLCF test harness.
3. [INDEX.md](../INDEX.md): every project by domain, with verification status.
4. [RULES.md](../RULES.md): evidence tags and scope rules.
5. [batches/](../batches/): per-batch summaries, oldest first.
6. [projects/](../projects/): one plain-English brief per project.
7. [verification/log.md](../verification/log.md): every fact check.
8. [connections/map.md](../connections/map.md): how projects relate.
9. [discovery/catalog.csv](../discovery/catalog.csv): master list.

The research was produced by a small pipeline of AI research assistants (discovery, reading, fact checking, connections, organizing) directed and reviewed by the maintainer. Role names such as "Scout", "Reader", or "Fact Checker" in the files refer to those pipeline stages, described in [roles.md](roles.md).
